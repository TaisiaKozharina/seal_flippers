- Meaning of the metadata marks on XRay images
  - XRay machine, program [VetView](https://vetview.org/). The seal data (DOB, gender, species, age) are manual inputs that they enter manually in VetView.
  - ID convention comes from museum: year that the animal came to the museum, then sequential number per the given year. No information about type of animal or its features is encoded.
  - (Extra) For fetus in Excel they record just the binary flag (its a fetus) and its length (in centimeters).
